    5. Frequency response scan (stepped sine)
    
    
    
      Plays a known sine tone at each of 18 test frequencies (40 Hz-2000
      Hz), one at a time, and measures the microphone level while each
      tone plays — a real driven measurement, not a guess from ambient
      noise. Takes about 15 seconds. Keep the phone still and quiet
      surroundings for a meaningful result.
    
    
    
    
      Workflow: run this scan once in a neutral/open space and tap
      "Save this scan as baseline" below. Then come back (same page,
      baseline is saved on this phone) and run the scan again in the room
      you want to check — this list will then show, per frequency, how
      many dB louder or quieter the room scan is versus your saved
      baseline. Large positive values are room-effect candidates; this is
      NOT a calibrated measurement and device drift/position also affect
      it.
