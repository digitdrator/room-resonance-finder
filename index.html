Room Resonance Finder

Plays a sweeping tone through the phone speaker, records the response, mathematically recovers the impulse response, and checks which frequencies keep "ringing" longest after the sweep stops — that decay behavior, not loudness, is what identifies a resonance.

1. Microphone

Other apps' audio may sound degraded while the microphone is active — this is an Android/Chrome system behavior, not specific to this page. Disable the microphone when you are not actively measuring.

3. Room sweep measurement

    

      40 Hz-4000 Hz exponential sweep, ~3s, plus ~2.5s of silence to catch
      the ring-down. Keep the phone still and the room quiet. Total

Decay curves — top candidates

    

      Level over time for the frequencies that decayed slowest after the
      sweep stopped (the "tails"). A flatter line = more ringing = more
      likely a resonance. 
    

    

    

      A single sweep cannot tell a stable room resonance apart from a
      transient noise source. Repeat the measurement and check whether
      the same candidate keeps showing up before trusting it.
