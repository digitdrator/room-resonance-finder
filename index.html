    5. Baseline comparison (Phase 4, exploratory)
    
    
    
      A baseline is a reference capture of this same phone — ideally in a
      neutral/open space. It helps separate peaks that belong to the phone
      itself from peaks that only show up in the room, but it is NOT a
      calibrated speaker measurement. Peaks matched to baseline are more
      likely device coloration; unmatched peaks are more likely room- or
      position-specific — neither is certain.
